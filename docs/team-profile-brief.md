# Your Zippily profile

We're building the new website and the client portal, and your profile appears
in both. Rather than us writing something on your behalf and getting it
slightly wrong, here's what we need from you.

**Takes about 15 minutes.** Send it back however suits — email, doc, Slack.

## Where this actually appears

Worth knowing, because it changes how you'd write some of it:

| Where | What shows |
|---|---|
| **About Us page** | Everything below — the full profile |
| **Blog posts you write** | Photo, name, role |
| **Zippily Sessions you host** | Photo, name, role, LinkedIn |
| **Client portal** | Name and role, on the card showing who's looking after that client |

That last one matters most. On a client's portal your name and role sit under
"your specialist" — it's the answer to "who is actually accountable for our
account". Write the role for that reader.

---

## 1. Name

As you want it published.

## 2. Pronouns

e.g. she/her, he/him, they/them. Your bio is written in third person, so this
saves anyone guessing.

## 3. Role — 2 to 6 words

Client-facing, not your employment contract title. Seniority plus the thing you
actually do works well.

> Sean's is: **Managing Director / Senior HubSpot Specialist**

"Consultant II" is a job grade. "Senior HubSpot Specialist" tells a client what
they're getting.

## 4. Photo

A shoot is being organised so these are consistent — mismatched selfies look
worse across a team page than no photos at all. If you'd rather supply your
own, it needs to be:

- Head and shoulders, portrait orientation
- Plain, uncluttered background
- Looking at the camera
- Good natural light, no heavy filters
- High resolution — it gets cropped for different sizes

## 5. Bio — 40 to 70 words

Third person. What you do, who you help, what you're genuinely good at.

> **Sean's, as a reference:** "Sean is the owner and founder of Zippily as well
> as an experienced Auckland based HubSpot specialist. He particularly enjoys
> marketing automation and understanding how businesses from different
> industries actually operate. Sean is passionate about automating processes and
> finding value at various touchpoints along the customer's journey with your
> business."

Avoid "passionate about delivering outcomes" and similar — every agency site
says it, so it says nothing. Be specific about the work.

## 6. Outside work — one sentence

The bit that stops the page reading like a corporate directory.

> **Sean's:** "Outside of work you'll find him running his monthly movie club or
> getting a game of social football in."

## 7. Skills — 3 to 6 short items

Renders as a tag row, so keep them short. Think "Marketing Hub", "Data
migration", "Reporting & dashboards" — capabilities, not adjectives.

## 8. Why you love HubSpot — a few words

The **area** you enjoy most.

> **Sean's:** Marketing Automations

## 9. Favourite HubSpot feature — one specific thing

Not the same as above. One actual feature.

> **Sean's:** Meeting Notetaker

These two are the most Zippily-ish fields on the page. Four people naming four
different favourite features says more about the team than any amount of
"passionate about client outcomes" — so pick a real one, even if it's
unglamorous.

## 10. LinkedIn URL

Full profile URL.

---

## What we're not asking for

Your direct phone or email. Client contact runs through
**hello@zippily.co.nz** so nothing depends on one person being at their desk.

## What happens next

Sean loads these into Sanity. Everything updates in one place and flows to the
website and the portal automatically — so if your role changes later, it's one
edit, not a hunt through pages.
